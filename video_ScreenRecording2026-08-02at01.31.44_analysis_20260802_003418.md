Based on the video provided, here is a detailed description of the "Achievements & Certifications" section of the portfolio website, focusing on your specific points:

**1) Card Layout (Tilt/Angle)**
The cards are arranged horizontally in a scrollable carousel (indicated by the "DRAG TO SCROLL" text in the top right). 
**Crucially, the cards are not flat against the screen.** They feature a distinct, static 3D perspective tilt. They are rotated along the Y-axis so that the left edge of every card appears slightly closer to the viewer (larger), while the right edge recedes slightly into the background (smaller). This gives the entire row an angled, architectural look, similar to viewing a row of dominoes from a slight angle. 

**2) Card Size**
The cards are uniform in size, utilizing a vertical, portrait-oriented rectangular shape. They are large enough to comfortably house a category tag at the top left, a prominent 3D icon in the center, a bold title, a subtitle (institution name), and a date tag at the bottom left, with ample negative space inside the card to prevent clutter.

**3) Hover Effect**
When the cursor hovers over a card, the following animations and changes occur:
*   **Glowing Borders:** A bright, neon-style border illuminates around the entire card.
*   **Color Coordination:** The color of the hover glow is dynamic and matches the specific category of the card. For example, hovering over the "AWARD" card creates a neon orange border; the "HACKATHON" card creates a neon green border; and the "CERTIFICATION" card creates a neon cyan/blue border.
*   **Corner Accents:** Thin, futuristic bracket accents (like targeting reticles) in the top-right and bottom-left corners also illuminate in the corresponding neon color.
*   **Tilt Remains Static:** Notably, **the hover effect does not change the 3D tilt of the card.** The card does not pop forward or flatten out to face the user; it maintains its angled perspective while the glowing border activates.

**4) Overall Design Style**
The website utilizes a modern, "dark mode," futuristic, and slightly sci-fi/cyberpunk aesthetic. 
*   **Background:** Deep black with a subtle, sparse pattern of white dots, resembling a starry night sky or a digital grid.
*   **Typography:** Clean, modern sans-serif fonts for the main text, with monospaced or technical-looking fonts used for the smaller tags (like "JAN 2024").
*   **Visuals:** Instead of flat graphics, the cards use vibrant, 3D-rendered emojis/icons (a gold medal, a rocket ship, a bar chart) that pop against the dark background. 
*   **UI Elements:** The use of neon glows, targeting brackets, thin border lines, and high-contrast colors (orange, green, cyan against black) heavily leans into a tech-focused, developer-centric design language.
